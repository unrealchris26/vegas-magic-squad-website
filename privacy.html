Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

The short version: the only personal information we collect is what you type into the booking form. We use it to answer your enquiry. We do not sell it, and there are no trackers or advertising cookies anywhere on this site.

Who we are

Vegas Magic Squad [CONFIRM: registered legal entity name], Las Vegas, Nevada. For anything in this policy, write to unrealvegas@gmail.com or call (207) 458-3115.

What we collect

Only what the booking form asks for, and only when you choose to send it:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, including the country code you select
  • Your event date, event type, approximate guest count and venue or city
  • Anything you write in the free-text box

Two technical details travel with the form as well: the page address you sent it from, and the time you sent it. Nothing else about your visit is recorded.

Only your name and email address are required. Every other field can be left blank, though the more you tell us the fewer emails it takes to give you a real answer.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to book us, to arrange the booking. That is the whole purpose. [CONFIRM: whether enquirers are also added to a marketing or newsletter list. If they are, this policy must say so plainly and offer a way to decline.]

Who else sees it

When you submit the form, it is sent to GoHighLevel (HighLevel, Inc.), the customer relationship system we use to manage enquiries. They process it on our behalf and store it on their servers. Their own privacy terms apply to that storage.

That is the only third party your form data reaches. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to advertisers.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixels, and stores nothing in your browser. The typefaces, the animation library and the video all load from this site's own server rather than from a third party, so simply reading these pages tells nobody anything about you.

The one external link is to our Instagram profile in the footer. Following it takes you to Instagram, where Meta's own policies apply.

How long we keep it

[CONFIRM: retention period. A common approach is to keep enquiries for the length of the booking conversation plus a defined period afterwards — say 24 months — then delete them. Pick a period the squad will actually honour, because a stated period that is ignored is worse than none.]

Your choices

Write to unrealvegas@gmail.com and we will, on request:

  • tell you what we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete it
  • stop contacting you

You do not need to give a reason, and asking costs you nothing. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; we will honour those requests too.

Children

This site is aimed at people booking entertainment, not at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us their details, tell us and we will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not apply a material change retrospectively to information you have already sent us.
