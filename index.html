Questions we get asked

The things planners ask us before they book

What kinds of events do you perform at?

Corporate events and private parties. Company parties and holiday dinners, conference receptions, product launches, trade-show stands, weddings, milestone birthdays and house parties. Anywhere a room of people has drinks in their hands and time to be surprised.

Can we book one performer, or is it always all three?

Either, and you do not have to work out which. Book one of us or all three — Brandon on cards, Kent on mentalism, Dream on classical magic. Tell us what the event is and we will tell you honestly what it needs, including when the answer is “fewer of us than you were about to book”.

What is the difference between strolling and a stage set?

Strolling means we come to your guests — moving between tables and groups during a reception or dinner, working a handful of people at a time. A stage or parlour set is everyone seated, watching one act together. Most evenings are better with both: strolling while people arrive and eat, a set once they have settled.

What do you need from us on the night?

For strolling, genuinely nothing — no stage, no sound, no setup, and no change to your running order. We arrive, we work, you carry on. For a seated set we will go through space, sound and lighting with you once we know the venue, and we will tell you well in advance if anything needs arranging.

How much does a booking cost?

There is no fixed price list, because an hour of strolling at a drinks reception and a full evening with all three of us are not the same job. Send the date, the event type and a rough headcount through the form and we will come back with a real number rather than a range.

What happens when I submit the form?

It sends us an enquiry, not a confirmed booking — nothing is held and no date is reserved until we have replied and agreed the details with you. You are not committing to anything by asking, and we would rather you asked early than found out too late that the date had gone.
