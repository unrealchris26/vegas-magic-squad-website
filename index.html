Corporate parties & receptions

Your guests are already mid-conversation, and the last thing you need is someone interrupting it. So we don’t. We come to the tables through the drinks hour and dinner service, work a few people at a time, and leave each group with something to tell the next one.

Nothing has to stop, nothing gets announced, and the run sheet you spent three weeks on stays exactly as you wrote it.

  • We come to your guests, so the evening never pauses
  • No stage, no seating plan, no running order to rearrange
