About Kent

My name is Kent Axell. Search me out on YouTube to see some of my work, and watch the hello video I’ve put together.

My work has been featured from off-Broadway stages to national television. You may have seen some of it on the Emmy Award-winning National Geographic program Brain Games, or featured on New York One, or Fox5.

I’ve put on over 200 shows for celebrities and the public in a single year, including my own recurring mentalism and magic show Determined — an experience in mind reading never to be forgotten — which plays frequently throughout the year in Manhattan theaters.

I also direct and perform in the highest rated dinner and magic show in New York City, A Taste of Magic.
